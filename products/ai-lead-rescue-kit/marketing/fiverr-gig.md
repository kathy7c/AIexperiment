## Gig description

Are leads getting lost in your inbox, form submissions, or messy spreadsheets?

I will set up a lightweight AI lead tracking workflow using n8n, OpenAI, and Google Sheets so every new inquiry is captured, qualified, and ready for follow-up.

What the workflow can do:

```text
- capture new leads from a form or webhook
- extract name, email, company, and message
- classify leads as Hot / Warm / Cold / Spam
- summarize the customer need
- generate a copy-paste-ready follow-up email draft
- recommend the next action
- save everything into Google Sheets
```

This is best for:

```text
small service businesses
solo founders
consultants
coaches
local businesses

freelancers
```

I keep the setup simple and maintainable. No heavy CRM required.

## FAQ

### Do you send replies automatically?

No. I create suggested replies for human review. Auto-send can be discussed after testing.

### Can this work with Gmail?

Yes, Gmail can be added as a custom workflow.

### Do I need a CRM?

No. This setup uses Google Sheets as a lightweight CRM.

### Can you connect Typeform, Tally, or Webflow?

Yes, if the tool can send a webhook or connect through Zapier/Make.
