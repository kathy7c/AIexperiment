## Description

Most small businesses do not need a heavy CRM on day one.

They need a simple way to capture every inquiry, understand who is serious, and know what to do next.

AI Lead Rescue Kit is a ready-to-import n8n workflow that turns form submissions into clean Google Sheets CRM rows. It uses OpenAI to summarize the lead, classify it as Hot / Warm / Cold / Spam, suggest a follow-up reply, and recommend the next action.

## FAQ

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. You need to import the n8n workflow, connect credentials, and paste your Google Sheet ID.

### Does this work with Gmail?

The base version is webhook-first. Gmail can be added as a custom setup.

### Does it send emails automatically?

No. It generates a suggested reply for human review.

### Can I customize the lead scoring?

Yes. Edit the prompt in the `Build OpenAI Prompt` node.

### Can I use this for clients?

Yes, if your license terms allow client delivery. Add your own license terms before publishing.
