# Upwork Pitch Templates

## Best-fit jobs to target

Search for:

```text
lead capture automation
Google Sheets CRM
n8n automation
Zapier Google Sheets
AI follow-up
workflow automation
form to Google Sheets
CRM follow-up automation
```

Avoid:

```text
full-stack SaaS
machine learning model training
complex backend
VPS / Docker ownership
frequent meeting requirements
vague enterprise automation
```

## Short pitch

```text
Hi, I can help you turn this into a lightweight lead capture and follow-up workflow using n8n, OpenAI, and Google Sheets.

The core setup I would suggest:
1. capture new leads from your form/webhook
2. use AI to summarize and qualify each inquiry
3. classify leads as Hot / Warm / Cold / Spam
4. save everything into a Google Sheets lead tracker
5. generate a suggested follow-up reply for review

I already have a working template for this flow, so I can adapt it to your tools instead of starting from scratch.

Quick question: where do your leads currently come from: website form, Gmail, Typeform, Facebook Lead Ads, or another source?
```

## More specific pitch for Google Sheets CRM jobs

```text
Hi, this sounds like a good fit for a lightweight Google Sheets CRM automation.

I can set up a workflow that captures each new lead, logs it into Google Sheets, uses AI to summarize the need, assigns a Hot/Warm/Cold status, and creates a suggested follow-up reply so nothing gets missed.

I would keep the first version narrow:
- one lead source
- one Google Sheet
- AI qualification
- follow-up draft
- clear handoff notes

That keeps the workflow easy to test and maintain.

What is the current lead source you want to automate first?
```

## Pitch for n8n jobs

```text
Hi, I can build this as a simple n8n workflow.

My suggested structure:
- trigger from form/webhook/Gmail
- normalize lead fields
- OpenAI step for summary and qualification
- Google Sheets append/update
- optional Slack/email alert for hot leads

I focus on keeping the workflow readable, documented, and easy to adjust later. I can also include a short handoff note so you know how to change the prompt and sheet columns.

Can you share one example lead message and the Google Sheet columns you want?
```

## Important positioning

Do not oversell as:

```text
AI agent
autonomous CRM
full sales automation
```

Sell it as:

```text
simple lead rescue workflow
AI-assisted follow-up
Google Sheets CRM automation
human-reviewed reply drafts
```
